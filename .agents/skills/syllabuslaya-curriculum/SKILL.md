# Syllabusलय Curriculum Management

## Verification rules

1. Identify the institution, programme title, applicable curriculum version/effective year, and academic structure before adding content.
2. Prefer primary sources: official university faculty/department, CTEVT, government examination/curriculum bodies, or official curriculum documents. Recognized institutions may help locate documents but must not replace primary-source verification when one exists.
3. Verify every code, subject title, term/year placement, mark, credit/hour value, assessment rule, practical component, and prerequisite independently from the correct version.
4. Keep distinct degrees, majors, universities, and curriculum versions separate. Do not merge curricula into a plausible-looking but unofficial programme.
5. Do not invent requested learning outcomes, chapters, textbooks, pass marks, or practicals. If source detail is unavailable, mark that field as awaiting verification and link the official document.

## Content model

- Preserve the site's existing hierarchy: level → programme → semester/year → subject → unit/chapter → topic/subtopic → resource.
- Keep annual and semester structures as stated by the institution; do not convert one to the other for visual convenience.
- Attach source metadata to the specific programme or subject and make the version/source visible to students.
- Ensure search indexes only present, source-backed data and direct results to the correct term and subject.

## Review checklist

- Compare all additions to the source document, including numbering, terminology, subject codes, and marks.
- Check that existing programme content is unchanged.
- Clearly label any unverified or incomplete details.
- Add regression checks for structure, counts, source links, and direct search routing.
